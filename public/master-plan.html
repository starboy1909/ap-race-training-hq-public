THE PROGRAMME LOGIC

Hybrid periodisation, not elite-athlete cosplay.

Marathon performance, HYROX specificity and maximal hypertrophy cannot all peak in the same week. The plan keeps every quality alive while rotating the primary emphasis around the confirmed race campaign.

The hierarchy is simple: availability first, aerobic durability second, Pro-load station execution third, and muscularity maintained throughout. Added work has to earn its recovery cost.

SUCCESS ORDER
1Train consistently
2Build aerobic durability
3Close Pro-station gaps
4Retain muscularity
5Peak for the right race

TWO-A-DAY RULE

Use a maximum of two split days in a normal week. If recovery or movement is abnormal, remove the second session before removing sleep, the long run or the primary quality session.

INTENSITY RULE

At least 70% of run time remains easy. HYROX compromised running counts as intensity. Sub-4 pace is 5:41/km, but it enters in controlled segments, not every run.

Substitutions can maintain fitness, but they do not replace sled, wall-ball or transition skill. High-load posterior-chain work returns only after normal movement and the relevant progression gates.

DIRECT TRUNK DEVELOPMENT

Load the abs. Stop chasing hundreds.

CORE A

Cable crunch 2–3×8–12
Pallof press 3×10/side

CORE B

Hanging knee raise 3×8–12
Ab wheel 3×6–10

OPTIONAL C

Suitcase carry 3×30–40 m/side
Dead bug 2×8/side

Use six to ten challenging direct sets per week. Exhale through the hardest part, keep one to three repetitions in reserve, and stop before technique breaks.

Progress load or clean repetitions slowly. High-repetition trunk flexion is not required for abdominal development.

RECOVERYSLEEP FIRST

Shorten optional work when sleep opportunity is insufficient.

ENERGY-AVAILABILITY RULE

Falling performance, persistent soreness, sleep disruption or recurrent niggles are reasons to increase recovery and energy intake, not to add more training.

READINESS ENGINE

The plan changes before the body breaks.

GREEN

Normal movement, normal energy and stable recovery. Build as planned.

AMBER

Lingering soreness, low recovery or mild symptoms. One hard session; reduce volume 10–20%; no second workout.

RED

Altered movement, increasing pain or illness. No hard run or HYROX density; use recovery and clinical guidance.
